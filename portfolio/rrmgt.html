The Challenge

RR Manpower needed a credible online presence that could convert both job seekers looking for placements and companies searching for staffing solutions — two very different audiences with different needs.

The Solution

We designed a clean, professional two-path homepage where visitors immediately self-select as either an employer or candidate, leading them into tailored conversion funnels with relevant content and call-to-actions.

Key Engineering Features

Specific architectures built to solve RR Manpower Services's unique business requirements.

Employer-Candidate Split Flow

Dual-path homepage design guiding each visitor type into their own personalised journey and conversion funnel.

Industry Sector Pages

Dedicated service pages for each industry vertical (manufacturing, logistics, IT) with sector-specific content.

Direct Enquiry System

Simplified contact forms and WhatsApp integration enabling rapid response to both placement and staffing enquiries.
