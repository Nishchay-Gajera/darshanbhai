The Challenge

The resort needed to bypass third-party booking fees by driving direct reservations. The website had to convey the luxury of the resort while offering a seamless, secure, and intuitive booking engine.

The Solution

We crafted a visually stunning, immersive web experience that transports users to the safari before they even arrive. Integrated with a custom, secure booking API, the platform successfully shifted the majority of their reservations to direct channels.

Key Engineering Features

Specific architectures built to solve Baboon Safari Resort's unique business requirements.

Direct Booking Engine

Real-time room availability and secure payment processing.

Virtual Tour Integration

Embedded 360-degree views of luxury tents and safari vehicles.

Weather API Integration

Live local weather updates to help guests pack and plan activities.
