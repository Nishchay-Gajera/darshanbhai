The Challenge

In a highly competitive local event planning market, Pacific Events needed a visually captivating portfolio to showcase their past work while streamlining the lead capture process for high-end weddings and corporate events.

The Solution

We designed an immersive, image-forward web interface that immediately engages visitors. By implementing a sticky conversion funnel and optimizing the media delivery network (CDN), the site loads blazing fast while maintaining stunning visual fidelity, resulting in a 40% bump in booking inquiries.
