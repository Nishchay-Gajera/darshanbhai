The Challenge

Aavya Life Science required an enterprise-grade digital platform to showcase their wide array of services including Food & Feed testing, Clinical Diagnostics, and contract research. They needed a secure portal to share compliance certifications and manage business inquiries efficiently.

The Solution

We designed a high-trust, structured corporate site featuring dedicated technical service pages, clear navigation for NABL and ICMR accreditations, and integrated lead generation forms optimized for enterprise clients and B2B engagement.

Key Engineering Features

Specific architectures built to solve Aavya Life Science's unique business requirements.

Research Service Directory

A robust service catalogue detailing technical capabilities across various domains like genomics, biotech, and food science.

Compliance & Accreditations

A dedicated portal section providing downloadable NABL and ICMR certifications, ensuring high trust for enterprise clients.

B2B Lead Generation

Optimized inquiry funnels for contract research and laboratory testing services to streamline client onboarding.
