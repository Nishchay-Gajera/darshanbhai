The Challenge

Government job seekers in India face fragmented information across slow, outdated websites. NearMe Job aimed to be a centralized, lightning-fast platform to aggregate vacancies and syllabus details.

The Solution

We engineered a highly scalable, SEO-optimized content architecture. By focusing on mobile-first responsiveness and accelerated page speeds, the platform quickly climbed organic search rankings, serving thousands of concurrent users reliably during major exam announcements.

Specific architectures built to solve NearMe Job's unique business requirements.
