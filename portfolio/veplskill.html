The Challenge

VEPL Skill aimed to digitize their training programs and make them accessible to a wider audience. The challenge was to create an intuitive and engaging learning environment that could accommodate various course types and student interactions.

The Solution

We designed and developed a comprehensive EdTech platform with seamless Learning Management System (LMS) integration. The resulting platform offers a user-friendly interface for both instructors and learners, facilitating effective skill development.
