The Challenge

WhereElse Properties needed a compelling digital showcase for a new township development, presenting complex property data — floor plans, pricing tiers, amenity maps — in a visually compelling and trustworthy format.

The Solution

We built a stunning real estate microsite with interactive property browsing, an integrated Google Maps display of the development site, downloadable floor plan PDFs, and a lead registration portal for interested buyers.

Key Engineering Features

Specific architectures built to solve WhereElse Properties's unique business requirements.

Interactive Property Listings

Filterable property catalogue with unit type, size, price, and availability status updated in real-time.

Development Map Integration

Interactive Google Maps layer overlaying the township layout, nearby schools, hospitals, and transport routes.

Downloadable Floor Plans

High-resolution floor plan PDFs for each unit type, downloadable after a simple lead capture form submission.
