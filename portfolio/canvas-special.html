The Challenge

Canvas Special needed an e-commerce experience that felt as premium as their product. Customers needed to visualise their photos on canvas before purchasing, and the checkout had to support local South African payment methods.

The Solution

We built a bespoke WooCommerce store with a custom photo-to-canvas configurator, allowing customers to upload images, preview the final product in a room mockup, and select size/frame options before adding to cart.

Key Engineering Features

Specific architectures built to solve Canvas Special's unique business requirements.

Live Canvas Configurator

Real-time photo upload and preview tool showing customers exactly how their image will look as a finished canvas print.

SA Payment Gateway

Integrated with local South African payment providers including EFT and credit card processing for smooth transactions.

Nationwide Delivery Engine

Dynamic shipping calculator with real-time courier rates and estimated delivery windows across South Africa.
