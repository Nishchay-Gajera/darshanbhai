The Challenge

As a growing EdTech institute, Bright Digital Spark needed a robust platform to manage course enrollments, present curriculum details, and handle student onboarding efficiently without relying on third-party aggregators.

The Solution

We developed a customized Learning Management System (LMS) frontend that integrates seamlessly with their enrollment workflows. The vibrant, dynamic design appeals to a younger demographic, driving higher engagement and a significant increase in online course registrations.
