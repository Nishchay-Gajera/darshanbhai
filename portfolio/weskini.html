The Challenge

Weskini required a digital storefront that mirrored the premium quality of their physical products. The challenge was balancing high-resolution product imagery with blazing-fast load times to prevent cart abandonment.

The Solution

We architected a headless e-commerce solution. By decoupling the frontend and optimizing the critical rendering path, we achieved sub-second load times while delivering a luxurious, app-like shopping experience.
