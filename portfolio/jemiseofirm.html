The Challenge

Jemi SEO Firm needed a digital presence that could match the bold promises of their brand — "$100K Revenue Generated", "10X ROI", and "95% Client Retention". Their previous website failed to convey authority or convert visitors into enquiries. They needed a site that built immediate trust and drove action.

The Solution

We built a conversion-focused agency website on WordPress, architected for speed and lead capture. The homepage was structured to guide visitors through a value journey — social proof, service highlights, and clear CTAs. Technical SEO foundations were baked in from day one, ensuring the site could rank for competitive local and national keywords.
