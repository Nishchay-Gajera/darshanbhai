The Challenge

Umiyaram 101 needed a digital presence that could serve their diverse community — announcing events, managing pilgrimage registrations, and sharing religious content — in a respectful and accessible manner.

The Solution

We created a community-first website with event calendars, pilgrimage registration forms, multimedia galleries, and multilingual content support to serve devotees both locally and internationally.

Key Engineering Features

Specific architectures built to solve Umiyaram 101's unique business requirements.

Event Calendar & Registration

Full event management with registration forms, seat capacity tracking, and automated confirmation messages.

Multimedia Gallery

Rich photo and video gallery showcasing religious events, festivals, and community gatherings.

Multilingual Support

Content available in Gujarati, Hindi, and English to serve the broad community audience.
