The Challenge

Pro Albums Direct needed a sophisticated product configurator where professional photographers could specify exact album dimensions, paper stocks, cover materials, and page counts — all before placing an order.

The Solution

We developed a multi-step product builder on WooCommerce with conditional pricing logic. The UI guides customers through every personalisation option with live price updates, making complex B2B orders intuitive and error-free.

Key Engineering Features

Specific architectures built to solve Pro Albums Direct's unique business requirements.

Multi-Step Album Builder

Guided product configurator for selecting album type, size, paper, cover, and quantity with real-time price updates.

Photographer B2B Portal

Dedicated trade pricing section for professional photographers, with bulk order discounts and account management.

Premium Gallery UI

Luxurious, high-resolution product showcase with lifestyle photography demonstrating finished album quality.
